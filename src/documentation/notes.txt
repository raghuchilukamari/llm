 --------------------------------------------------------

Fine Tuning  - Adjust all parameters in training a pretrained LM to adapt to a specific problem
             - requires computational power - rarely used

Low-Rank Adaption - By Microsoft researchers
            - Adapt to specific task by reducing computational resources
            - Significantly reduces number of params to train by applying low rank approximations to layers
            - Low GPU
            - QLoRA

            Supervised Fine Tuning -
                doing standard fine tuning on pretrained LLM with a smaller data
                still computationally intensive
            RLHF -
            - Trained to follow human feedback over iterations

 Paramter Efficient fine Tuning (PEFT) - HuggingFace
            - Freezes most of pretrained params and fine-tunes only small number of additional params
            - outperform SFT
            - tiny checkpoints, less storage (highly portable)
            - supports LoRA and Prompt Tuning

 PEFT Library - LoRA, Prefix Tuning, P-Tuning, QLoRA
